# Bonjoro

> Bonjoro is a personal-video customer engagement platform (a wholly owned brand of Vimily Pty Ltd,
> Sydney, Australia). Teams record short one-to-one videos — "Bonjoros" — and send them to customers at
> moments that matter: signup, onboarding, purchase, renewal, churn risk. The product also runs a video
> testimonial collection surface. Bonjoro publishes a REST API (API V2, 123 operations) covering
> greets, recipient profiles, campaigns/workspaces, recordings and media, message templates, results
> and engagement stats, transmissions/deliverability, replies, teams and users, custom sending domains
> and signatures, and the trigger/action automation engine that connects Bonjoro to CRMs and email
> platforms.
